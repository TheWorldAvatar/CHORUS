# Competency Questions

## 1. Risk assessment and classification

- **CQ1.** Which areas are classified under different shrink-swell risk levels (e.g., high, medium, low)?
  - *Note:* "regional statistics" mode considered following our meeting at the end of the conference + this question
- **CQ2.** What is the shrink–swell risk level of a specific location?
  - *Note:* Yes, the risk score will be defined by the intersection of two parameters (calculated via the service): definition of local risk (intersection of datasets - "aggravating factor") + probability of triggering it (at different time steps - according to climate changes) "triggering factor")
- **CQ4.** Which risk indicators are available for assessing shrink–swell risk in a location?
  - *Note:* In France, only one indicator is used, based on BRGM data, characterizing only the subsoil risk factors: high, medium, low. A second indicator, used by insurers, complements the BRGM indicator by combining it with a drought indicator (SWI). For the service, we envision several factors, as mentioned in the answer to the question above: a predisposing factor, a triggering factor (with different time horizons), and a risk factor combining the first two parameters.
- **CQ5.** What is the shrink–swell risk classification of soil using Jones classification?
- **CQ6.** How does shrink–swell risk evolve over time in a given location?
  - *Note:* Based on IPCC data, evolution of soil moisture levels (drought parameter increasing)
- **CQ7.** What are the projected shrink–swell risk levels under future climate scenarios?
